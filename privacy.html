Privacy policy

Kids Money Games · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short: the Kids Money Games collect nothing. There are no accounts, no adverts, no tracking, no analytics and no in-app purchases. A child's progress is saved only on their own device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Which games this covers

Coin Sorter, Play Shop, Price Detective, Party Planner and Pocket Money Month, in every form they come in:

  • the apps for Amazon Fire tablets, from the Amazon Appstore;
  • the files bought from our Etsy shop, opened in a web browser;
  • the website version at mmitache88.github.io/money-games.

What we collect

Nothing. The games do not ask for a name, age, email address, photo, location or any other personal information, and they have no way to send anything anywhere.

The Fire tablet apps do not have permission to use the internet at all, and ask for no other permissions. The web versions are locked down by the browser so that they cannot send anything over the internet: the website only downloads the games' own files.

What is saved on the device

To remember where a child is up to, each game saves on the device itself:

  • the levels played and the stars earned;
  • the grown-ups' settings, such as sound on or off;
  • on the website version, which games have been unlocked with their access code, and whether the tip about installing it has been closed.

This stays on the device. In the Fire tablet apps it is not included in cloud backups. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser's data for the web versions, removes it. A grown-up can also wipe it with Reset in the game's grown-ups area.

Exporting progress

The grown-ups area can export progress as a small file, to move it to another device. The file holds only the levels, stars and settings above. It is saved where the grown-up chooses, and is never sent anywhere by the game.

Children

The games are made for children, and are designed so that no information about a child ever leaves the device. The grown-ups area is behind a press-and-hold button, so children do not change settings by accident.

The shops and the website host

Buying a game is handled by the shop you buy it from. Amazon's and Etsy's own privacy policies cover the information they hold about a purchase, and we do not receive any information about children from them.

The website version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard logs of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. That is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. The games themselves add nothing to it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page, with a new date at the top.

If you have a question about privacy, contact us through the store where you bought the game: the Amazon Appstore listing or our Etsy shop.
